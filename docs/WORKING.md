## Literature (locked from discussion)

The world is high fantasy, and it is not this Earth. It is **dark**. It is **harsh**. Survival of the fittest is enough reason to fight. **Most things are evil. Few are good.** Do not complicate motive.

It has **races** (these are the **peoples**), **kingdoms**, **deities**, **cosmic beings**, **monsters**, **heroes**, **mythical beasts**, and wild **fauna and flora** (the Pokémon-like catalog — not peoples).

Familiar kinds exist only as *inspiration*. Elves, dwarves, dragons, and the rest are not Tolkien-Earth stock. The analogues are other. The three given so far are **examples, not the set**:

- Elf-like: beautiful, long-lived, **reptilian**.
- Dwarf-like: a **worm** people.
- Dragon-like: **humans** with that bearing — a different kind, taken after the idea.

The world is old. Known history for several races is on the order of ten million years. Some deities and cosmic beings date to this star system (~four billion years) or to this universe (~ten billion years). Extra **quantum fields** exist because the constants are not ours.

**Raw / primal fields** (named so far: **light**, **darkness**) are the most ancient.

**Elemental** and more **physical** fields sit in between: old, not as ancient as raw light/dark.

**Quantum emotion fields** appear once life **feels**. Most spells and powers tap here.

**Who taps raw:** in general, only **cosmic**, **elemental**, and **ancient** beings. **Exceptions exist.** No absolute hard constraint.

Emotion fields can attune to raw or elemental fields. **Every combination and permutation is possible.**

Races differ along this variance → affinities, specialisation, biomes, preferences, jobs, classes → different **defence**, **attack**, **speed of attack**, and other combat character.

**All lore has presence in the game.** Some of it is rare, niche, or **secret**. Several cards and items are **hard to unlock**, gated on in-game conditions.

Lore is not flavor text. **Concepts, attributes, card types, and values come out of this world.**

## Draft race codex (proposed — not locked)

The three analogues from discussion are included. The rest is a **wide, out-of-box draft** to cut, rename, or replace. Working names only. Same method: taken after a familiar kind, made other. Dark world; most of these should read as dangerous, not cute Earth mascots.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Veilscale | elf | Beautiful, long-lived, **reptilian**. |
| Burrowkith | dwarf | **Worm** people. |
| Emberkine | dragon / human | **Dragon-bearing humans** — the kind, not a costume. |
| Woundbrood | orc | People who **sprout from injuries**. Violence is how they continue. |
| Glasschoir | angel | Folded **angles of pale light**. Cruel, exact, bureaucratic. |
| Debtflame | demon | They burn what is **owed**. Kindness is fuel they steal. |
| Needlehost | fairy | A person as a **swarm of rust needles**. Pretty at a distance. |
| Slickborn | merfolk | Live in **film and tar-water**. Clean air drowns them. |
| Bonegrove | treant | They inhabit **forests of calcified dead**. The wood is ancestor. |
| Faceborrow | goblin | Small. They **wear the last face they took**. |
| Unfinished | undead | Not rot. They simply **did not stop**. |
| Hollowscript | vampire | **Paper-thin**, drink memory until the page is blank. |
| Moonmolt | lycanthrope | Each cycle they **shed a person**. The pelt walks. |
| Threshold | shadow | Only solid in **doorways and cuts**. |
| Saltmoth | moth / pilgrim | Fly toward endings. Leave **white dust kingdoms**. |
| Cathedral | hive insect | The city **is** the organism. Districts are organs. |
| Quietcap | fungus | The majority. They **fruit in order to vote**. |
