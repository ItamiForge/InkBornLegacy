## Literature (as given)

The world is high fantasy, and it is not this Earth. It has races and kingdoms, deities and cosmic beings, monsters, heroes, mythical beasts, and Pokémon-like creatures. Familiar kinds exist only as *inspiration*. Elves, dwarves, dragons, and the rest are not copied from Tolkien-Earth stock. The analogues are other.

Examples of that method (not yet named as finished races):

- Elf-like: beautiful, long-lived, **reptilian**.
- Dwarf-like: a **worm** people.
- Dragon-like: **humans** with that bearing — not Earth dragons wearing a human mask, and not Earth humans with horns as costume. A different kind, taken after the idea.

The world is old.

Known history, for several races, runs on the order of **ten million years**.

Deities and cosmic beings are older still. Some of them date to the origin of this **star system**, on the order of **four billion years**. Some date to the **beginning of this universe**, given as on the order of **ten billion years**.

The constants of this universe are not ours. There are **several more quantum fields** than a mundane physics would allow.

Some of those fields have existed since the start.

Some came into being only once **life started feeling**. Those are **quantum emotion fields**.

Most beings who work spells and powers do it by tapping the emotion fields.

Some beings can tap the more **primal and raw** fields themselves.

**Elemental** fields and more **physical** fields sit somewhere in between: old, but not ancient in the way **raw light** or **darkness** are ancient.

Emotion fields can **attune** or hold **affinity** to raw fields, or to elemental fields. **Every combination and permutation is possible.**

Races differ along this variance. From it come **affinities** and **specialisation**, the **biomes** they occupy, their **preferences**, **jobs**, and **classes**. Combat qualities follow: **defence**, **attack**, **speed of attack**, and the rest are allowed to differ because the lore differs.

The lore is not flavor text on top of a generic auto-battler. **Concepts, attributes, card types, and values should come out of this world.**

## Understanding (fields, without extra names)

A stack of reality, oldest at the bottom:

1. **Raw / primal fields** — present from the early universe (as given). Named so far: **light**, **darkness**. These are the most ancient. Few tap them directly.
2. **Elemental and more physical fields** — in between. Old, not as ancient as raw light/dark. Not listed yet.
3. **Quantum emotion fields** — younger. They appear when life feels. This is the usual source of spells and powers.

**Attunement:** an emotion field may lean toward a raw field, or toward an elemental/physical field, or (as stated) any permutation of those relationships.

**Who taps what:** most beings → emotion. some beings → raw/primal directly. Elemental/physical sit in the middle; who uses them as a primary tap is not yet specified beyond that placement.

**Races as mechanical engines:** racial field-variance → affinity/specialisation → biome, vocation, class → different attack / defence / attack speed (and other stats as they are named later).

**Peoples named only as method:** reptilian long-lived “elf” analogue; worm “dwarf” analogue; dragon-like humans. Also in the world, not yet sorted into card kinds: kingdoms, deities, cosmic beings, monsters, heroes, mythical beasts, Pokémon-like creatures.

Nothing else in this file is a finished race list, pantheon, map, or type chart.
